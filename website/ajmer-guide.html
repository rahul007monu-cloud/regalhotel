Dargah Sharif

Dargah of Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti (R.A.)

The Dargah of Hazrat Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti, known as Khwaja Garib Nawaz, is one of the most visited shrines in South Asia. People of every faith come here to offer prayers, chadar and flowers. Regal Hotel is about 400 m away, so you can walk to the Dargah for every prayer.

Tips for ziyarat

  • Cover your head inside the Dargah. Caps and scarves are sold outside.
  • Wear modest clothes. Shoes are left at the shoe stands near the gates.
  • Keep your phone, wallet and bags safe; the lanes get very crowded.
  • Chadar and flowers can be bought from shops on the way. Agree on the price first.
  • Thursday evenings and Fridays are the busiest. Early mornings are calmer.
  • Timings change by season. Our reception will tell you the current timings.

Urs Sharif

Visiting during Urs

Urs marks the death anniversary of Khwaja Garib Nawaz and is held every year in the Islamic month of Rajab. Lakhs of zaireen come to Ajmer for these days, so hotels near the Dargah are booked weeks ahead.

📅
Book earlyReserve 4–6 weeks before Urs with a small advance.
🚶
Walk, don't driveRoads near the Dargah are closed to traffic on main days.
💧
Stay lightCarry water, keep valuables in the hotel and travel in groups.
