FAQ

Common questions

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in and check-out are at 12 noon. Early check-in or late check-out can be arranged on request, subject to availability.

Which ID do I need at check-in?

Every adult guest needs a valid government photo ID (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Driving Licence or Passport). Foreign guests need a passport and valid visa.

How far is the hotel from Dargah Sharif?

About 400 metres, a short walk along Dargah Road.

Is parking available?

Yes, free on-site parking is available for guests.

Do I need to pay in advance?

A small advance confirms your room, especially during Urs, Pushkar Mela and weekends. The balance is paid at check-in.

Are rates higher during Urs?

Yes, demand is very high during Urs and Pushkar Mela. Message us on WhatsApp for the exact rate for your dates.
