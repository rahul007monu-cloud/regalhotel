01Executive Summary

Regal Hotel has a strong advantage: it is a short walk from Dargah Sharif and about 1 km from Ajmer Railway Station. Demand is steady all year and rises sharply on Thursdays and Fridays, during Urs Sharif and during Pushkar Mela.

Many guests, especially pilgrims and families, decide late and contact the hotel by phone or WhatsApp. A large share of bookings also come through Booking.com, MakeMyTrip, Goibibo and Agoda, each charging a commission on every room night.

We propose a set of automations that:

  • Launch the hotel's own website in English and Hindi, so guests who search "hotel near Ajmer Dargah" can find Regal Hotel and book direct.
  • Answer every guest enquiry instantly on WhatsApp, 24 hours a day, and take bookings with UPI advance payment.
  • Move more bookings to direct, saving OTA commission on each one.
  • Keep room availability and rates in sync across all booking sites, preventing overbooking, with event-based pricing for Urs, Pushkar Mela and weekends.
  • Improve the guest experience: online pre-check-in, QR room service from Riviera Restaurant, faster housekeeping.
  • Raise online ratings by sending happy guests to Google and alerting the manager privately about unhappy ones.
  • Give the owner a daily WhatsApp report and a live dashboard of rooms, revenue and staff.
Live demo available. A working, clickable demo of these modules has been prepared with sample data. It shows exactly how the system will look and behave for Regal Hotel before any investment is made.

05Proposed Solution: 9 Modules

1. WhatsApp Booking Bot

Replies instantly in English and Hindi. Shares rates, photos, location and checks live availability. Takes a UPI advance and sends the booking confirmation and invoice. Hands complex chats to reception.

2. Channel Manager Sync

One place to update rooms and rates. Booking.com, MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Agoda and the WhatsApp bot stay in sync, so a room sold on one channel closes on the others.

3. Event-based Pricing

Suggested rates for every date based on Urs, Pushkar Mela, Thursday/Friday demand and occupancy. The owner approves with one tap; rates go live everywhere.

4. Pre-check-in & Form C

Guests upload ID before arrival on WhatsApp. Check-in takes under 2 minutes. Form C details for foreign guests are prepared automatically.

5. QR Room Service

A QR code in each room opens the Riviera menu. Orders go to a kitchen screen and are added to the room bill automatically.

6. Housekeeping & Maintenance

Check-out creates a cleaning task for the least busy staff member. Repair tickets (AC, geyser, TV) go to maintenance on WhatsApp. Room status updates live.

7. Review & Feedback Automation

Feedback request after check-out. 4–5★ guests are sent to Google Reviews; 1–3★ guests alert the manager privately. AI-drafted replies to public reviews.

8. Owner Dashboard & Daily Report

Live room map, occupancy, revenue, booking sources and ratings. A summary is sent to the owner's WhatsApp every night.

9. Hotel Website (English + Hindi)

Regal Hotel does not have its own website today. We build a fast, mobile-first site on the hotel's own domain: home, rooms & rates with a price calculator, "Book on WhatsApp", Ajmer ziyarat & Urs guide, map and contact. It is set up for Google search (local SEO, hotel schema, Google Business Profile link), so guests can find the hotel and book direct without paying OTA commission.
